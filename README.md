# 🤖 AI Awareness Quiz 2026 — 60 Questions & Answers

## 📌 About the Project

This repository contains **60 AI awareness questions and answers** designed to help students and beginners learn about Artificial Intelligence (AI), Generative AI, and modern AI technologies.

The content is organized into six sections covering fundamental AI concepts, popular AI tools, model terminology, and AI safety.

## 📚 Topics Covered

1. **AI Companies & Latest Models** — Popular AI companies and their models.
2. **Generative AI & LLM Concepts** — LLMs, prompts, tokens, inference, and fine-tuning.
3. **Agents, RAG & Modern AI Development** — AI agents, RAG, embeddings, APIs, and MCP.
4. **AI Image, Video, Audio & Famous Tools** — Image generation, video generation, TTS, STT, and OCR.
5. **Coding AI, Hardware & Practical Awareness** — AI coding assistants, GPUs, NPUs, and model distillation.
6. **Latest AI Awareness & AI Safety** — AI model awareness, prompt injection, and responsible AI usage.
